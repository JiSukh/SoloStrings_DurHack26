Chord by ear (microphone)

Most reliable: the laptop listens to the strings and works out the chord from the notes ringing. Just fret the chord and let the robot strum; it follows within about a bar. No training needed.

Chord camera (left hand)

Point the laptop camera at the fretboard. Teach each chord once: pick it, hold the shape, press Record. Then the detected chord is sent to the strummer automatically.
